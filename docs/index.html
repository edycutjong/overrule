Overrule /verify

AI health-insurance appeals dept — the signed decision ledger, made witnessable.

The appeal it could not prove, it refused to send.

Overrule is an offline, deterministic appeals department for denied health-insurance claims. Every draft is byte-matched against the plan document before it can leave the machine, and every decision — including every refusal — is Ed25519-signed, SHA-256 hash-chained, and re-verifiable from the export alone. This page is that ledger, opened up for inspection.

Read this first: nothing on this page is a production claim. Everything below is synthetic fixture data regenerated by the offline self-test — no live payer, no real member, no mail actually sent, nothing deployed as a product, no customers and no revenue. Overrule is self-help document tooling: it is not legal or medical advice and promises no outcome.
